## Creative tooling community

> choTaku treats scenes as canonical and panels, shots, comics, manga pages, storyboards, and videos as projections. It is designed for creators who need continuity, identity memory, layout contracts, and reproducible asset lineage across multimodal outputs. The current example is a Crimson Curse Master storyworld: https://github.com/AvaTar-ArTs/choTaku
